This binder holds the board's records for one building.
- Bylaws, declarations and rules go in governing; agendas and minutes in meetings; statements and budgets in finances; quotes and work orders in maintenance; letters and email in correspondence.
- Name minutes and agendas "<yyyy-mm-dd> <meeting>".
- Only requests the board must answer or decide become to-dos; one to-do per request, never one per reminder.
